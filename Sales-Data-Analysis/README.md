# Sales Data Analysis

## Project Summary

This project analyses the Sample Superstore sales dataset using Python to understand sales performance, profitability, product categories, sub-categories, regions, discounts, and monthly trends.

The analysis includes data inspection, missing-value and duplicate checks, date-based analysis, sales and profit analysis, profit margin calculation, and data visualization.

## Dataset

- Sample Superstore dataset
- 10,194 rows
- 21 columns

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Key Findings

- November had the highest total sales.
- December had the highest total profit.
- February had the highest profit margin.
- April had the lowest profit margin.
- Technology generated the highest total profit among categories.
- Chairs had the highest total sales among sub-categories.
- Copiers generated the highest total profit among sub-categories.
- West had the highest overall sales and profit among regions.

## Analysis Performed

- Loaded and inspected the dataset
- Checked missing values and duplicate records
- Analysed sales and profit by category
- Analysed sales and profit by sub-category
- Analysed regional performance
- Analysed discounts and quantities
- Extracted year and month from order dates
- Analysed monthly sales and profit
- Calculated monthly profit margins
- Created sales and profit visualizations using Matplotlib
- Identified business insights from the analysis

## Key Learning

This project helped me understand how Python and Pandas can be used to analyse real-world sales data and turn raw data into meaningful business insights.

It also helped me understand that high sales do not necessarily mean high profit, and that profit margin provides additional insight into business performance.
